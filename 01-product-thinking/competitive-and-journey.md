# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Priya, The Overwhelmed Browser (long-tenured heavy subscriber)
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Sit down in the evening and quickly land on something she genuinely wants to watch.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Scrolls for 20 minutes in a 15,000-title catalog without finding anything and closes the app.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Physical DVDs (external fallback) and manual endless scrolling/scanning in the home feed.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Opens the app intending to watch a movie. 2. Manual scrolling across home rows for ~20 minutes. 3. Fails to find anything desirable among 15,000 titles. 4. Closes the app in frustration. 5. Switches to a physical DVD or comfort re-watches.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Spending 20 minutes scrolling through an overwhelming catalog of 15,000 titles and finding nothing she actually wants to watch, resulting in zero viewing time.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** XR-01 (Priya): "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD."
